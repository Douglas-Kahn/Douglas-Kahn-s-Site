Douglas Kahn

Douglas Kahn is a pen name.

He writes about human desire, civilization, technology, and the questions that emerge as artificial intelligence reshapes the human world.

He does not believe that personal details such as education, occupation, age, nationality, or social position are necessary to read his work. Such information may instead create unnecessary preconceptions.

His ideas are meant to be read and judged on their own, rather than through the authority or identity of the author.
