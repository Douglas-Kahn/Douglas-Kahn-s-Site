What is AI to humanity?
How far can I go with AI?

The questions I posed to AI began to connect through dialogue, and those connections gave rise to new questions. I am testing whether this cycle—thought giving rise to questions and questions, in turn, expanding thought—can be called “Reasoning Amplification,” and whether human–AI interaction itself can generate new knowledge.

I remain endlessly curious about how AI will change the world, how it will change us, and what we must preserve—and what we must change—to remain human.
